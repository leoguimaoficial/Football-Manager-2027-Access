------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

under BepInEx\core and BepInEx\unity-libs.

            -> licenses\Prism\ (NOTICE and the individual license texts)

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mono C# compiler (mcs.dll) — SOURCE BUNDLE ONLY, not in this package
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Listed for completeness: mcs.dll is NOT installed on a player's machine. It ships only
inside FM26Access-v<version>-source.zip, where it is needed to compile the dev driver's
/eval endpoint. Its absence here is why /eval cannot compile anything on a player install.

  Upstream: https://github.com/sinai-dev/mcs-unity (a Unity build of Mono.CSharp)
  License:  MIT X11, taken from the compiler's dual MIT X11 / GNU GPL license
            -> lib\mcs-LICENSE.txt in the source bundle

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Microsoft.Web.WebView2
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Powers the companion window that displays the accessible page your screen reader
reads. Support DLLs live in BepInEx\plugins\FM26Access\companion\.

  Project:  https://developer.microsoft.com/microsoft-edge/webview2/
  License:  Microsoft Software License Terms (redistributable)
